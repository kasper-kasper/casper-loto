# Casper Grand flagship discussion — 2026-10-09

Status: concept study only; OD-047. Production remains unchanged.

Current production baseline read on 2026-10-09: 50,000 CAS ticket, 5bn CAS
base jackpot, Sunday 20:00 Europe/Moscow, 5/35 + 2/5. Owner requests a
purchase-screen design study and a polished 3D draw with no video inserts.
No larger catalogue placement is requested.

Owner's lower-tier proposal: 4+1=1m, 4+0=500k, 3+2=300k, 3+1=200k,
3+0=100k. Discussed 2+2 and 2+1 payouts equal to the 50k ticket price.
Assistant proposes senior tiers 4+2=3m, 5+0=10m, 5+1=20m; final values
remain undecided. Exact 5+2 shares the jackpot under the existing principle.
Each ticket receives its exact category once; a ticket-price prize does not
cancel its purchase or mark it REFUNDED.

Under uniform independent unordered draws there are C(35,5)*C(5,2)=3,246,320
outcomes. Using all proposed fixed tiers, expected ordinary payout is
7,320.60 CAS per ticket (14.6412% of 50,000), excluding jackpot and refill.
This is an expectation, not a funding guarantee or a bound on actual liability.
The shared Casper Fund pays ordinary prizes under the current funding model;
stress scenarios and fund coverage must be assessed before approval.

Visual directions: (1) Grand Hall: navy/champagne glass draw chambers and
metallic number medallions; (2) official Casper Loto brand: original horizontal
logo reference, navy/ivory/muted gold and a modern sculptural chamber;
(3) Golden Orbit: indigo observatory, gold rings, glass chambers and precision
controls. All concepts show 35 main buttons in five rows of seven plus five
secondary buttons, selected 4/13/21/28/35 + 2/5, a 50k ticket and random
purchase options. Generated images are visual studies, not functioning UI.
The actual approved logo asset must be used unchanged in implementation.
