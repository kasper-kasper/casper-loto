# Where I want to take Casper Loto

**English** · [Русский](ROADMAP.ru.md)

[← About the game](../README.md) · [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) · [Devlog](../DEVLOG.md)

Updated: 23 October 2026.

I am building Casper Loto on my own. This is my current plan, with target
dates I will refine as development progresses.

## Development plan

| When | Main focus | What I want to build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| October–November 2026 | Transparency centre | Public draw statistics, past results, probability explanations and an odds simulator in one place |
| November 2026 | Next-generation profiles | A personal collection showcase, all-time statistics, records, rare rewards and a profile style of your own |
| January 2027 — launch | First full season | A theme lasting roughly three months, with a collection, tasks, free progression and rewards |
| January 2027 | Public voting | Player suggestions and support for ideas inside the Mini App |
| February–March 2027 | Shared goals and communities | Cooperative challenges and each player's contribution to a shared result |
| May 2027 | Open API | Documentation and safe access for third-party tools |
| Summer 2027 | Lottery builder | A tool for putting together and proposing your own lottery |

If a feature takes more work than expected, I will revise the dates rather
than cut verification short. Bug fixes, translations and usability
improvements will continue between these milestones.

## 1. Next-generation profiles

I want each player to have something of their own: a personal history,
memorable results and a collection worth looking through.

- A personal showcase for your collection and selected rewards.
- Statistics covering your entire time in the game.
- A history of personal records and memorable game events.
- Rare rewards and a clear history of how you earned them.
- Themes and other ways to make your profile recognisable.

Badges and themes already exist. This step brings them together with your
history and statistics in a more personal space.

## 2. Transparency centre

I want to bring rules and probabilities together in one understandable place,
rather than leave them scattered across individual games.

- Public statistics for completed draws.
- Past results, with the rules that applied to each draw.
- Plain-language explanations of the chances of winning.
- A simulator showing how probabilities play out over a large series of
  virtual attempts.

The simulator will help explain probabilities through examples.

## 3. Seasons — first launch in January 2027

I want to create full game periods, not just holiday lotteries.
Each season will last roughly three months, with its own theme,
collections, tasks and rewards.

Progress will stay free: no paid pass, no paid advantages and no pressure
to log in every day. I want people to enjoy returning at their own pace.

I will define the exact calendar, tasks, rewards and treatment of unfinished
progress before the first season begins.

## 4. Shared goals and communities

Not everything needs to be a competition. I am interested in challenges
that players complete together — for example, a community completing
a certain number of different game challenges.

I want to show shared progress and each participant's contribution,
with room to take part without racing for first place. Community formation,
contribution tracking and rewards still need to be designed.

## 5. Public voting — January 2027

Players will be able to suggest ideas inside the Mini App, describe a
mechanic and explain what makes it interesting. Other players will be
able to discuss proposals and vote for the ones they support.
This can arrive before the full lottery builder.

I want to implement the ideas that attract the most support.
Before adding a game, I will check its mathematics, the clarity of its
rules and how it fits the virtual economy.

The proposal format, selection process and protection against vote
manipulation will be defined before implementation.

## 6. Lottery builder — summer 2027

After voting launches, I want to create a tool for putting together a
lottery proposal: choosing its format, describing its rules and setting
out its winning conditions. That should make an idea easier to present
to the community and prepare for implementation.

The builder's exact capabilities still need to be defined.

## 7. Open API — May 2027

I want other developers to be able to build useful tools for Casper Loto,
such as their own results visualisations, draw calendars or analyses
of public statistics.

I plan to begin with read-only public data, documentation and request
limits. Personal data, account access and CAS operations will not become
public. The endpoints and access conditions still need to be designed.

## Beyond this schedule

A standalone web version remains a longer-term idea.
The previous separate web client has been retired from production.
I have not set a new release date for it.
